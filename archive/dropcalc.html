How the numbers are worked out

Estimates only
  • Estimates, from the numbers you type and the rules below. Not tax, legal or accounting advice.
  • You pay for every piece you make. Production is counted for the whole run, sold or not. Defective pieces are paid for once and cannot be sold.
  • Pieces sold is sellable pieces times sell-through, rounded to whole pieces.
  • Discounts apply to every retail sale as an average. Wholesale pieces sell at the wholesale price with no discount, fees, shipping or returns.
  • A return refunds the piece but not the shipping. Payment fees are not handed back, and a returned piece is not counted as sold again.
  • Per-order amounts (shipping, postage, materials, the fixed payment fee) are split across the pieces in an average order.
  • Sales tax is shown as collected and passed on. It is never part of revenue or profit. The payment fee is charged on it.
  • Break-even is the smallest number of pieces sold, in your planned mix, at which net profit is zero or more. Production and one-time costs count as already spent.
  • Net margin is net profit divided by revenue. Revenue is sales after discounts and refunds, plus wholesale and shipping paid by customers.
